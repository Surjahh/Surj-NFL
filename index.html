How to read the edge

Transparent calculations, not black-box confidence.

Model probabilityEstimated chance of your side hitting
Break-even probabilityChance needed at the listed odds
Probability gapModel probability minus break-even
Expected valueEstimated net profit per $100 staked

EV is only as reliable as the probability model and the price. A positive estimate can still lose, and a weak model can create fake edges.
